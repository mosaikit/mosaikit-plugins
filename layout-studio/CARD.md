# Studio

A layout of the interface: an indigo band with the brand and a centred search, a grey sidebar with the apps in groups, a white work area and soft shadows. Each app has a 3D icon, and the colours follow the light and dark mode. The 3D icons are from 3dicons.co (CC0).

Uses:
- A friendly portal for many apps that people open all day
- Tenants that want a more colourful, product-like look than the default
- Teams that switch between light and dark mode and want both to look right
