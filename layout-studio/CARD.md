# Studio

A layout of the interface: an indigo band like the one of Teams (the brand over the sidebar, the button, the tenant and a centred search), a grey sidebar with the apps in groups that show how many apps they hold and an icon for each group when the sidebar is closed, a white work area and soft shadows. Each app has a 3D icon, and the Overview of the Tenant Manager gets a banner with 3D art, 3D figures and a picture on each panel. The colours follow the light and dark mode. The 3D icons are from 3dicons.co (CC0).

Uses:
- A friendly portal for many apps that people open all day
- Tenants that want a more colourful, product-like look than the default
- Teams that switch between light and dark mode and want both to look right
