# Whiteboard

A shared free-form board for the people you share it with: sticky notes, rectangles, ellipses, text, arrows and freehand drawing on an endless canvas. Everyone who has the board open sees the changes and the pointers of the others live, readers follow along without being able to change anything, and the board is saved in the item, so it is there the next time.

Uses:
- Brainstorming and retrospectives with sticky notes, during a call
- Flows, diagrams and quick sketches drawn together
- A visual plan of a workshop that people can come back to and export as PNG or SVG
