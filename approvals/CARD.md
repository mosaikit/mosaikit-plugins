# Approvals

A request that one or more people must approve or reject, with the history of who decided what and when. Choose the approvers and the rule (one approval is enough, everybody must approve, or one after the other), share the request with them, and each one decides once, with a comment. The state is always worked out from the decisions, so nobody can change the past.

Uses:
- Purchase requests that a manager and the finance office must sign off
- Holiday and remote-work requests with a clear answer and a reason
- Sign-off of a document by several people before it goes out
