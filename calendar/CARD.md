# Calendar

The agenda of the team. Write events with a day, a time and a repeat, invite people of the organisation and see everything by month, week or day. The invitees answer yes, maybe or no, and the open tasks with a due date appear next to the events, so deadlines and meetings are in one place. Times are local times, or at the right hour in any time zone you choose for the event, and one occurrence of a repeating series can be changed or deleted on its own.

Uses:
- The team agenda with meetings, invitations and answers everybody can see
- Shared holidays, absences and recurring events such as the weekly standup
- Deadlines from Tasks next to the meetings of the same week
