# Bookmarks

A shared collection of web links: each bookmark has a picture of its site, a title, an address, a note and tags. Search across all of them, filter by tag, mark favourites, and open or copy a link with one click. Each bookmark opens in a new tab or inside the page, in the content area (with a default for the list, and a new tab for the sites that forbid framing). When you save, the platform looks at the page once to fill in the title and picture you left empty. A bookmark can be shared with people and groups like any other item.

Uses:
- The links a team keeps coming back to: dashboards, wikis, vendor portals
- A reading list or a research collection built together and sorted by tags
- Onboarding links for new colleagues, shared with a whole group at once
- The dashboards of other systems, opened inside the portal next to your tools
