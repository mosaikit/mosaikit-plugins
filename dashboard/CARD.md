# Dashboard

One page you arrange from widgets that show what matters to you across the apps: your open and overdue tasks, notices waiting for your confirmation, polls to vote, requests to decide, your recent notes. Apps add their widgets by themselves, every widget shows only your own data, and a board can be shared. Keep several boards, one columns layout each, and see them refresh every minute.

Uses:
- A morning page with what is due today and what waits for your decision
- A team board shared with the people who follow the same work
- One place to start from instead of opening each app in turn
